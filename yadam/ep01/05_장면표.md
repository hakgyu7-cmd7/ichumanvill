## 1. 맨 앞 30초: Flow 영상 4개

순서: 시작 그림을 ChatGPT(또는 Flow)로 먼저 만들고 → Flow에 시작 프레임으로 올리고 → 움직임 지시문을 붙여 넣습니다.

공통 앞머리: `8-second shot. Keep the art style and every face exactly as in the start frame.`
공통 끝: `Audio: [소리] only, no voices, no music, no on-screen text.`
얼굴이 흔들리면 맨 앞에 `Minimal motion, the face stays exactly the same.`를 넣습니다.
클립 소리는 조립할 때 끄거나 아주 작게 둡니다.

시작 그림:

```text

Okbun (wearing a clean pale-cream jeogori and a deep plum chima) sits behind the table, frozen in shock. Lady Yun and Deoksu sit in the background. Medium shot over Deokbae's shoulder.
```

움직임:

```text
8-second shot. Keep the art style and every face exactly as in the start frame. The camera slowly pushes in over the son's shoulder toward the old mother. The son pushes the folded document a little further forward, his mouth moving as he speaks. The mother's hands tremble on her knees. Audio: quiet indoor ambience and distant market murmur only, no voices, no music, no on-screen text.
```
